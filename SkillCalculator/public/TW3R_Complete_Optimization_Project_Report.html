System / Skill Tree & Allocation Engine Mechanics & Scaling Formulas

Potent Sting Alchemy (3/3) Deals +15% damage with poisoned weapons (or +30% against poison-immune targets). Requires the weapon to carry an active poison capability (Poisoned Blades oil, Morana Runestones, or innate weapon stat).
