## 0. New Start Mod: `'A New Beginning'` — Starting Area (Level 1)

* **`[quest_reward] (story) (gear)` — Quest: `'A New Beginning'` → Select `(Cat School) Path`**
  * Grants the Feline Light Armor starting kit and fast-attack / critical-hit archetype.
  * **Remastered v5.01c Perk Rule:** **`Cat School Techniques`** (General Tree) is unlocked at **0 points invested**—slot it immediately for **+25% Critical Hit Damage** and **+5% Fast Attack Damage** per piece of Light Armor!
* **Pre-Combat Consumables & Pockets Checklist:**
  * **Consumables:** Equip **Food & Drink** (with CROW's *Regeneration* module, food buffs last **20 minutes** and stack—check healing factor) + **Tawny Owl** potion (+Stamina Regen) & **Beast / Specter Oils**.
  * **Pockets:** Equip **Torch** (pairs with the *Ladder with Torch v2.1.0* mod for cave and crypt exploration).
* **Opening Skill Progression Order (Levels 1–6):**
  1. **Skill #0 (Free 0-Pt Passive):** **`Cat School Techniques`** *(General — Slotted immediately)*
  2. **First Skill (`Lvl 1`):** **`Melt Armor`** *(Signs — Igni Tier 1: Weakens enemy armor up to 75% so Cat School Fast Attacks shred armored targets)*
  3. **Second Skill (`Lvl 2`):** **`Delusion`** *(Signs — Axii Tier 1: Rank 1–2 unlocks all early White Orchard & Velen dialogue checks for +25–40 XP each)*
  4. **CROW Combat Core (`Lvls 2–6`):** **`Muscle Memory (3/3)`** → **`Arrow Deflection (1/3)`** (+1s CROW stamina stack extension) → **`Resolve (1/3)`** → **`Fleet-Footed (3/3)`** (negates CROW's 50% dodge i-frame penalty while keeping 135% dodge speed) → **`Undying (1/3)`** → **`Counterattack (3/3)`** + **`Attack Is the Best Defense`**.
