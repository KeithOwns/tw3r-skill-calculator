1. - Arrive_White Orchard - (LEVELS 1 – 4)
⚠️ CUTOFF RULE: Complete ALL White Orchard side quests & Gwent before talking to Vesemir inside the tavern at the end of 'The Beast of White Orchard'.
================================================================================
[Lvl 1] [unmarked_reward] (story) — Yennefer's Crystal Bird Skull
  - Location: Starting campsite brush directly south of Woesong Bridge.
  - Action: Use Witcher Senses immediately to loot the skull; unlocks a bonus dialogue/cutscene with Yennefer later. Expires if you leave White Orchard!

[Lvl 1] [quest_reward] (story) — Lilac and Gooseberries (Axii Conversation Checks)
  - Location: White Orchard Inn.
  - Action: Use 'Delusion' (Rank 1) on the defensive peasants inside the inn (+25 XP) and on the 3 bandits outside (+25 XP).

[Lvl 1] [unmarked_reward] (gear) — Scholar Aldert Geert & Innkeep Elsa (Missable Gwent Cards)
  - Location: White Orchard Inn.
  - Action: Beat Aldert Geert at Gwent for 'Zoltan Chivay' card, and buy all 4 Gwent cards (including 'Decoy' & 'Foltest: Lord Commander') from Elsa BEFORE departing White Orchard.

[Lvl 1] [unmarked_reward] (gear) — All 6 White Orchard Places of Power (+6 Ability Points)
  - Locations: (1) South of Woesong Bridge (Quen), (2) North of Ransacked Village (Aard), (3) South of Mill in ghoul swamp (Igni), (4) Ford near Broken Bridge guarded by Level 6 Bear (Yrden), (5) White Orchard Cemetery crypt door (Axii), (6) Abandoned Site near Sawmill (Quen).

[Lvl 1] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — A Frying Pan, Spick and Span
  - Location: Old woman by Riverside Hut (West of Woesong Bridge).
  - Action: Find Thaler's scorched monocle inside the locked hut (burn the barred door with Igni). Cutoff: Leaving White Orchard.

[Lvl 1] [quest_reward] (gear) — Dirty Funds & Deserter Gold (Treasure Hunts)
  - Location: Bandit Camp NE of Sawmill -> Spy's Shanty N of Mill.
  - Action: Read the journal in the chest to unlock both treasure hunts for early relic swords & Crowns.

[Lvl 2] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Twisted Firestarter
  - Location: Willis (Dwarven Blacksmith in White Orchard village).
  - Action: Track the arsonist Napp down by the river behind the inn, use 'Delusion' (Rank 1) on him, and march him back to Willis for Crowns & smith access. Cutoff: Leaving White Orchard.

[Lvl 2] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — On Death's Bed
  - Location: Tomira's Herbalist Hut (East of Sawmill).
  - Action: Brew 'Swallow' (1x Drowner Brain, 5x Celandine, 1x Dwarven Spirit) to save Lena. Unlocks a follow-up scene at the Nilfgaardian Garrison in Velen. Cutoff: Leaving White Orchard.

[Lvl 2] [quest_reward] (story) — Missing in Action
  - Location: White Orchard Notice Board / Dune Vildenvert at Ransacked Village.
  - Action: Search the battlefield with Dune and His Dog (Hussar); convince Dune to spare the Nilfgaardian deserter Rordrik so both survive and settle at the Sawmill. Cutoff: Leaving White Orchard.

[Lvl 2] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Precious Cargo
  - Location: Merchant sitting by the campfire north of the Sawmill swamp road.
  - Action: Inspect the cart tracks/arrows to expose the fake merchant as a Temerian guerrilla; chase him down on Roach. Cutoff: Leaving White Orchard.

[Lvl 2] [quest_reward] (gear) — Scavenger Hunt: Viper School Gear
  - Location: White Orchard Cemetery Crypt (guarded by Level 7 Wraith) & Ransacked Village Bandit Ruins.
  - Action: Loot both Serpent Steel & Silver Sword diagrams and craft both Viper Swords at the Nilfgaardian Garrison Quartermaster before leaving White Orchard!

[Lvl 2] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Contract: Devil by the Well
  - Location: White Orchard Notice Board -> Odolan -> Abandoned Village.
  - Action: Read 'Yellowed Diary' in the house AND pull the well rope BEFORE jumping into the well for extra story dialogue with Odolan & Tomira. Rewards Noonwraith Trophy + Mutagen.

[Lvl 3] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — The Beast of White Orchard & The Incident at White Orchard
  - Location: Nilfgaardian Garrison (Peter Saar Gwynleve) -> Mislav -> Buckthorn -> Royal Griffin.
  - Action: Slay the Royal Griffin (+Griffin Mutagen & Vesemir's Crossbow). Refuse or accept Emhyr's coin later in Vizima.

[Lvl 4] [quest_reward] (gear) — Temerian Valuables (Treasure Hunt)
  - Location: Underwater beneath the northern bridge to the Nilfgaardian Garrison -> Mill cellar.

3. - Arrive_Velen (No Man's Land) - (LEVELS 4 – 11)
⚠️ PROXIMITY RULE: Never ride past a yellow '!' marker in Velen; engage immediately or the NPC dies/despawns permanently.
================================================================================
[Lvl 3] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Funeral Pyres
  - Location: Priest of the Eternal Fire West of Border Post / North of Mulbrydale.
  - Action: Clear early before Level 9 so XP doesn't grey out; save Timmon at the 3rd pit and confront the corrupt priest.

[Lvl 4] [unmarked_reward] (gear) — Looters & Highwaymen's Booty (Roadside '!' Events)
  - Location: Battlefield West of Inn at the Crossroads & Crossroads East of Crow's Perch.

[Lvl 5] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — The Nilfgaardian Connection & Bloody Baron
  - Location: Inn at the Crossroads -> Hendrick's Hut (Heatherton) -> Crow's Perch.
  - Action: Use 'Delusion' (Rank 1) or offer a drink to the Baron's men at the Inn to avoid bloodshed (unlocks peaceful pass into Crow's Perch instead of swimming the well). Beat the Bloody Baron at Gwent early for 'Sigismund Dijkstra'!

[Lvl 5] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Hunting a Witch & Wandering in the Dark
  - Location: Midcopse (use 'Delusion' Rank 2 on the villager behind the well) -> Keira Metz's Hut -> Elven Ruins.
  - Action: Equip Torch/Cat Potion & Tawny Owl; help Keira defeat Nithral (Wild Hunt Warrior).

[Lvl 6] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Magic Lamp
  - Location: Immediately inside the Elven Ruins at the end of 'Wandering in the Dark'.
  - Action: Agree to help Keira find the lamp! Puzzle order: Light the 4 braziers (3rd -> 2nd -> 4th -> 1st). Loot the Place of Power & Golem guarding treasures inside before leaving.

[Lvl 6] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — An Invitation from Keira Metz -> A Towerful of Mice -> A Favor for a Friend -> For the Advancement of Learning
  - Location: Keira's Hut -> Fyke Isle.
  - ⚠️ CUTOFF ORDER: Complete 'Lynch Mob' (Lvl 7, Hanged Man's Alley) BEFORE starting 'A Favor for a Friend'!
  - Action: At the end of 'For the Advancement of Learning' on Fyke Isle, convince Keira to go to Kaer Morhen (take Alexander's notes peacefully; never let her go to Radovid or fight her!).

[Lvl 6] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Forefathers' Eve
  - Location: Dock outside Fyke Isle (The Pellar approaches you immediately after finishing 'A Towerful of Mice').
  - Action: Meet the Pellar at the Fyke Isle stone circle at Midnight; protect the ritual from Witch Hunters, then defeat the restless ghost of the Pellar's father in the swamps.

[Lvl 6] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — The Fall of the House of Reardon & Ghosts of the Past (Letho Recruitment!)
  - Location: Lindenvale (Dolores Reardon) -> Reardon Manor.
  - Action: Enter the large barn WITHOUT breaking the tripwires, climb the ladder to the loft, and trigger 'Ghosts of the Past'. Help Letho fake his death against the bounty hunters and send him to Kaer Morhen! Also loot Dolores's lockbox inside the cellar ('unmarked_reward').

[Lvl 6] [unmarked_reward] (gear) — Harassing a Troll (Friendly Rock Troll Boris)
  - Location: Riverbank NW of Inn at the Crossroads.
  - Action: Slay the Nekkers attacking Boris before he dies; he rewards you with an Alchemy/Crafting diagram.

[Lvl 6] [unmarked_reward] (story) (gear) — Fake Papers & Thou Shalt Not Pass
  - Location: Border Post (Shady Merchant & Redanian Bridge Guard).
  - Action: Speak to the bridge guard first to log 'Thou Shalt Not Pass', then buy the pass from the Shady Merchant using 'Delusion' (Rank 1) and complete 'Bitter Harvest' (Lvl 9 — keep Alghouls off the scavengers!).

[Lvl 6] [unmarked_reward] (story) — Hazardous Goods (Proximity '!')
  - Location: Road between Inn at the Crossroads & Blackbough (or NW of Lindenvale).
  - Action: Help Gert Borel burn the plague-infected corpses with Igni immediately when the '!' spawns.

[Lvl 6] [quest_reward] (gear) — Contract: Woodland Beast
  - Location: Notice Board at Border Post -> Bridge to Graden.
  - Action: Let Vernossiel's Scoia'tael commandos keep the supplies for a free Transit Pass (or fight them for elf loot).

[Lvl 6] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Ladies of the Wood, The Whispering Hillock & Ciri's Room
  - Location: Crookback Bog -> Downwarren -> Whispering Hillock -> Crow's Perch.
  - Action: Loot 'The Ladies of the Wood' book in Ciri's Room at Crow's Perch (cutoff: Ugly Baby).

[Lvl 7] [quest_reward] (story) — Lynch Mob (Proximity '!' Cutoff)
  - Location: Fast Travel to Hanged Man's Alley.
  - Action: Intervene to save the Nilfgaardian deserter from the peasants BEFORE starting Keira's 'A Favor for a Friend'.

[Lvl 7] [unmarked_reward] (story) (gear) — Death By Fire & At the Mercy of Strangers (Proximity '!')
  - Location: Burning barn NE of Oreton (save the elf woman from bandits -> unlocks hidden chest under the tree!) & Riverbank West of Boatmakers' Hut (save John Verdun from drowners).

[Lvl 7] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Wild at Heart, A Greedy God & The Truth is in the Stars
  - Locations: Blackbough (Niellen the Werewolf), Ruins NW of Wastrel Manor (Sylvan 'Allgod' cellar illusion), and Benek (Old Sage — give him Dragonroot from the cave & play Gwent!).

[Lvl 7] [quest_reward] (story) — A Barnful of Trouble (Proximity '!')
  - Location: Carsten (Village NE of Oxenfurt).
  - Action: Slay the ghouls inside the locked barn before they kill the villagers.

[Lvl 7-8] [quest_reward] (gear) — Contract: Patrol Gone Missing (Lvl 7) & Contract: Shrieker (Lvl 8)
  - Locations: Army Group 'Center' Camp (Wyvern) & Crow's Perch (Cockatrice).

[Lvl 9] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Family Matters & Return to Crookback Bog
  - Location: Crow's Perch -> Oxenfurt -> Downwarren -> Crookback Bog.
  - ⚠️ CUTOFF: 'Return to Crookback Bog' permanently fails if you start 'Ugly Baby'! Complete it right at Level 9.

[Lvl 9] [unmarked_reward] (story) (gear) — A Dog's Life & Caravan Attack (Proximity '!')
  - Locations: Road East of Drudge (save the dog from wolves immediately!) & Southern Road near Frischlow.

[Lvl 9] [quest_reward] (gear) — Of Dairy and Darkness
  - Location: Novigrad Shopkeeper (Gildorf) -> Aeramas' Abandoned Manor (East of Oxenfurt).
  - Action: Navigate the cheese labyrinth to loot the 'Emmentaler' relic sword and unlock the portal leading to the hidden Place of Power (+1 Skill Point)!

[Lvl 10] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Fools' Gold, Defender of the Faith & Love's Cruel Snares
  - Locations: Lurtch (save Yontek & the pig villagers), Velen Shrines (don't walk away from the vandal students at the 3rd shrine!), and Forest SW of Condyle.

[Lvl 10] [quest_reward] (gear) — Contract: Jenny o' the Woods (Midcopse) & Contract: The Merry Widow (Lindenvale)
  - Action: Rewards Nightwraith Mutagen & Grave Hag Mutagen.

[Lvl 11] [quest_reward] (gear) — Scavenger Hunt: Griffin School Gear (Basic)
  - Location: Dragonslayer's Grotto (West of Downwarren) & Lornruk Lighthouse (NW Velen).
  - Action:Also loot the 'Superior Beast Oil' chest deep in the Lornruk underwater cave!

8. - Arrive_Toussaint (Blood and Wine) - (LEVELS 34 – 50+)
================================================================================
[Lvl 34-35] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Envoys, Wineboys -> The Beast of Toussaint -> Blood Run -> No Place Like Home (Corvo Bianco) -> Turn and Face the Strange
  - Action: Unlock the Corvo Bianco Vineyard Estate (+1,000 HP Bed buff, Armor/Weapon displays) and complete 'Turn and Face the Strange' immediately to unlock the Blood and Wine Mutation Chamber ('Euphoria' / 'Cat Eyes' / 'Mutated Skin' + 4 Bonus Skill Slots)!

[Lvl 37] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — ★ Father Knows Worst ('Brothers Quest' — Crossbow Bolt Vendor Unlock!) ★
  - Location: Notice Board -> The Clever Clogs Tavern (North of Lebioda's Gate) -> Albertus Cavern.
  - ⚠️ CRITICAL CHOICE: When Hugo's two brothers confront you outside Albertus Cavern after you rescue Hugo, select **"No one dies today"** so the three brothers reconcile peacefully!
  - Reward: **Hugo Monnart** opens his shop at **The Clever Clogs Tavern** and becomes the **ONLY vendor in the entire game who buys Crossbow Bolts** (plus Monster Trophies and Junk) at **100% full item value**! (If you let Hugo fight his brothers, the tavern business collapses and he never becomes a merchant.)

[Lvl 37-40] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Warble of a Smitten Knight, There Can Be Only One (Aerondight) & Scavenger Hunt: Grandmaster Feline Gear (Lvl 40)
  - Action:
    1. Prove all 5 Chivalric Virtues (Generosity, Honor, Compassion, Valor, Wisdom) to claim **Aerondight** from the Lady of the Lake (`[quest_reward] (gear)`).
    2. Craft the **Grandmaster Feline Set** (Lvl 40) at Lazare Lafargue in Beauclair (`[quest_reward] (gear)`).
    3. Clear the Arthach Palace Ruins Hanse Base (NE Toussaint) to loot the **Crafted Toussaint Knight's Steel Sword** diagram (`[unmarked_reward] (gear)` — #1 Steel Sword in the game: +300 AP, +100% Crit DMG, +20% Crit Chance)!

[Lvl 40-49] [quest_reward] (story) (gear) — Capture the Castle -> The Night of Long Fangs -> Beyond Hill and Dale... -> Tesham Mutna -> Pomp and Strange Circumstance -> Be It Ever So Humble...
  - Action: Choose 'Beyond Hill and Dale...' (Land of a Thousand Fables) to rescue Syanna, buy the Ribbon from the Little Flint Girl, and read all 5 entries in the Nursery Governess's diary before speaking to Syanna in 'Pomp and Strange Circumstance' to achieve the best Toussaint reconciliation ending!
