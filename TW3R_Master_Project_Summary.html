Key v4.0+ Skill Mechanics Engine Math

  • 3-Rank Normalization: Combat, Signs, and Alchemy skills max out at 3 ranks (down from 5), with tier gates at 0 / 6 / 12 / 18 points.
  • Battle Frenzy (3/3): Grants +9% Crit Chance per Adrenaline point (+27% flat Crit Chance at 3 points) stacking additively with Adrenaline damage.
  • Potent Sting (3/3): +15% damage with poisoned weapons, or +30% damage against poison-immune foes (requires active poison source).
  • Medium Armor Perk Stacking: Equipping 4 Medium pieces (or Balance enchantment) simultaneously activates full 4-piece bonuses for Griffin, Manticore, and Viper School Techniques.
  • Crossbow & Bombs: Anatomical Knowledge adds 10% of Silver Sword DPS (+430.2 flat dmg); Volatile Compound adds +0.3% bomb dmg per Toxicity (+64.2% at 214 Tox); Advanced Pyrotechnics grants 100% self-bomb immunity.
