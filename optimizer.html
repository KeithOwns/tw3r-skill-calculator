Combat Ergonomics & Input Flow

Why rear paddle dodging transforms Death March combat

Input Tactics

The Standard Controller Dilemma

In the standard controller layout, dodging requires shifting your right thumb from the right camera stick to the face button [B]. During those fractions of a second, camera tracking stops—leaving you blind to flanking wolves, charging foglets, or off-screen archers.

The 8BitDo P1 Paddle Advantage

By binding P1 on the back of the grip to B, your middle finger handles dodge execution while both thumbs remain permanently welded to the analog sticks. You track enemies continuously through evasions and parries without losing situational awareness.
