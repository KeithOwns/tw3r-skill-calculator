1. Optimal Execution Roadmap (Level 8 → Level 12)

Zero-Failure Path

Phase 1: Immediate Low-Level Cleanup (Lvl 8)

  • Contract: Devil by the Well (Lvl 2, White Orchard) — Only contract that fails at Isle of Mists.
  • Funeral Pyres (Lvl 3, Velen) — Clear immediately before XP greys out at Lvl 9; confront corrupt priest.
  • Contract: Woodland Beast (Lvl 6, Velen) & Contract: Patrol Gone Missing (Lvl 7, Velen).

Phase 2: Reardon & Keira Metz Chain (Lvl 8–9)

  • The Fall of the House of Reardon (Lvl 6) — Avoid barn tripwires; climb loft ladder to trigger Ghosts of the Past (Lvl 6, recruit Letho).
  • An Invitation from Keira Metz (Lvl 6) → A Towerful of Mice (Lvl 6).
  • Walk off Fyke Isle dock to meet the Pellar & complete Forefathers' Eve (Lvl 7).
  • A Favor for a Friend (Lvl 6) → For the Advancement of Learning (Lvl 8 — send Keira to Kaer Morhen).

Phase 3: Finalize Velen Arcs (Lvl 9–10)

  • Wild at Heart (Lvl 7), The Truth is in the Stars (Lvl 7), A Greedy God (Lvl 7), and Contract: Shrieker (Lvl 8).
  • Return to Crookback Bog (Lvl 9) — Hard cutoff before Ugly Baby.
  • Defender of the Faith (Lvl 10) & Fools' Gold (Lvl 10, Lurtch).

Phase 4: Griffin Gear & Novigrad Entry (Lvl 10–12)

  • Scavenger Hunt: Griffin School Gear (Lvl 11, Velen) & Of Dairy and Darkness (Lvl 9, Novigrad).
  • Play Olivier at the Kingfisher Inn (Gwent: Playing Innkeeps) before advancing Triss's quests!
  • Decompress The Nobleman Statuette (Lvl 14) with Triss before starting Now or Never.

Gwent: Playing Innkeeps — Novigrad / Velen Olivier Death Olivier (Kingfisher Inn, Novigrad) dies during Now or Never if you help the mages escape. Play Olivier and buy every Gwent card in his inventory immediately when entering the Kingfisher!
Defender of the Faith 10 Velen Proximity (3rd Shrine) Riding away after the Oxenfurt students spawn at the 3rd shrine south of Mulbrydale fails the quest. Complete at Level 9–10; stay and confront the students at the 3rd shrine.
Following the Thread 11 Novigrad / Skellige Fails at Ugly Baby Because Lambert's quest is already active in your journal, starting Ugly Baby before finishing it auto-fails the quest. Finish with Lambert in Novigrad & Faroe before starting Ugly Baby.
Black Pearl 13 Novigrad / Skellige NPC Death Fail Once Nidas dives into the water near Arinbjorn in Skellige, drowners swarm him on the shore. Leaving or reacting slowly kills Nidas. Sprint to the shoreline as soon as Nidas surfaces and kill the drowners immediately.
The Nobleman Statuette 14 Novigrad Blocked at Now or Never Requires Triss Merigold. If you complete Now or Never first, Triss leaves Novigrad until late Act III (Blindingly Obvious). Decompress with Triss (along with The Soldier Statuette) before starting Now or Never.
