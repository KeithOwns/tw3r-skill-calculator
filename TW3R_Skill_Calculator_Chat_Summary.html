🐺 Next-Gen (Patch 4.0+) Game Engine Math

1. Skill Tree Normalization

In Patch 4.0, skill nodes were rebalanced from 5 ranks down to 3 ranks maximum:

  • Combat (20 Skills): Fast, Strong, Defense, Crossbow, Battle Trance.
  • Signs (20 Skills): Aard, Igni, Yrden, Quen, Axii (Standard & Alternate forms).
  • Alchemy (20 Skills): Brewing, Oils, Bombs, Mutation, Trial of the Grasses.
  • General (15 Skills): Cat/Griffin/Bear School Techniques, Gourmet, etc. (1 rank, no tier gate).

2. Tier Unlock Thresholds

Tier 1 = 0 pts • Tier 2 = 6 pts • Tier 3 = 12 pts • Tier 4 = 18 pts spent in that tree.

3. Active Loadout & Mutagen Synergy

  • 12 Active Ability Slots: 4 quadrants (3 slots each).
  • 4 Mutagen Sockets: Greater Mutagens grant base +10% Attack Power (Red), +10% Sign Intensity (Blue), or +600 Vitality (Green). Each matching color slotted skill adds another base bonus (up to 4x).
  • Synergy Perk Multiplier: Rank 1 (+15%), Rank 2 (+30%), Rank 3 (+50%) extra mutagen power!
  • Blood & Wine Mutations: 12 mutations (Euphoria, Piercing Cold, Bloodbath, etc.) unlocking up to 4 bonus ability slots.
